About Sonification V2

Live Sound Camera & Audio-Visual Databending Studio

Concept & Architecture

Sonification V2 turns pictures and live camera feeds into sound. The app reads pixel data directly from your screen or webcam, feeds it into an audio synthesizer rack as raw signal, and paints the sound back onto the image in real time.

Philosophy & Intent

Pixels and audio samples are just bytes. When you feed an image into a synth filter, you hear textures and edges as tones and noise. When you route that audio back into the picture, the sound bends the frame. It is a two-way loop where visual art and electronic music run on the exact same signal wire.

Live Optical Feed

Stream smartphone or desktop optical sensors into continuous transduction loops. Use Send to phone to transfer session parameters via QR code.

Modular DSP Rack

Chain modules including diode ladders, state-variable filters, Karplus-Strong physical models, Buchla-style wavefolders, and delay lines.

Patch Sharing

Persist parameter configurations, save study revisions, inspect community patches, and export raw recordings.
